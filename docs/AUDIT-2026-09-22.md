# Interaction audit, 22 Sep 2026

Walked as a person would rather than read as source: every route at phone
width (402x874, Luna's account, real data), then the main flows pressed
through by hand in Playwright - tonight, a recipe and cook mode, the stock
list and an item, quick adjust, add item, the shopping list, the planner,
every sheet that opens. Every server action was held open and never answered,
so the screen shows what a person sees the moment after pressing and nothing
reached the database.

Ranked by how much a person would feel it. Time is a build estimate.

## Findings

### 1. Sheets open underneath the buttons pinned to the screen · ~30 min

The recipe page's ⋯ menu opens behind "Done — cooked for 15" and the tab bar:
the button sits across the middle of the sheet, over "Who can see it". The
sheet is `z-50`, but it renders inside the photo header's `absolute z-10`
layer, so it can never rise above the cook bar (`z-20`) or the tab bar
(`z-40`). Measured with `elementFromPoint`: the top element at the sheet's
middle is the Done button.

`Sheet` does not portal. Rendering it into `document.body` fixes this one and
every future one, and retires the trap written up beside `.stagger` - a
transformed list item stops trapping a sheet when the sheet is not inside it.

### 2. "Cook it" means two different things · ~15 min

On Tonight, "Cook it" takes the stock off the shelves in one tap when the app
can see every line, and opens the recipe when it cannot. "Cook it" reads as
"start cooking", so the likely moment to press it is before cooking, and the
stock goes then. The recipe page calls the same act "Done — cooked for 15".
One act, two labels; one label, two acts.

### 3. Back links ignore where you came from · ~30 min

The item page's "← The shelf" always goes to `/tonight`, which opens on the
recipe card with the shelf off-screen. Open Dark soy sauce from the `/pantry`
list, press back, and you land on a different screen at the top, having lost
your place. "← Stock" on the shopping list goes to `/pantry`, which is not a
tab any more. `/cooked` says "← Recipes" where the tab says Cookbook.

### 4. The shopping list's name box is blank · ~10 min

When the kitchen has stock (always, in practice) the box is a `SoftSelect`
with no placeholder and no label: an empty field with a ▼ that looks like a
dropdown you cannot type into. The fallback plain input, which almost nobody
sees, has "What do you need?". The new-recipe ingredient box is the same
component with the same blank.

### 5. Baking powder's substitute is red pepper powder · ~30 min

The recipe offers "Instead of baking powder: Red Pepper Powder". The name
matcher scored the shared word "powder" past `NAME_FLOOR`. One of these on
screen is enough to stop anybody trusting the substitute button. The name
score should need the thing's head noun or more than one generic word
(powder, sauce, stock, flakes, paste).

### 6. Ingredients you do not have start ticked as "used" · ~30 min

Every line starts ticked, including Baking powder and Raisins marked "To buy"
and Raisins marked optional. The line above says "Ticked ones come off your
shelves". A person reads a tick on a thing they do not own as the app being
wrong. And the tick is a 24px circle only, not the row, which is small for
floury hands.

### 7. The shelf says nobody gave an amount, for things with an amount · ~20 min

Unsalted butter shows as a "?" outline, under "the outlines are things nobody
has said the amount of" - and the stock list says 410g. It is an outline
because there is no pack size, so no level can be drawn. The sentence is
wrong for every row like it.

### 8. Quick adjust's step size reads as the amount · ~10 min

"Whole Milk 900ml open [−] 100 [+]": the 100 is how much each press moves,
and it sits where a reader expects the amount. The stock list's own stepper
labels it "± 100 ml"; quick adjust does not.

### 9. The item page · ~45 min

The name is printed twice, as the page title and again as the card title
directly under it. Dates are raw ISO ("Was good until 2026-09-17"). The page
has two separate Save buttons, packaging and details, so editing both and
pressing one loses the other.

### 10. An empty week puts its help text after 21 empty rows · ~10 min

"Nothing planned yet. Tap a day to put something in it…" is below Sunday.
Nobody scrolls past 21 blank slots to read how to fill them.

### 11. Discover shows the same recipes twice · ~30 min

Carrot, squash & pear soup, Turkey ragu, the flapjacks and Creamy pesto pasta
each appear under "Cooks trust these" and again under "From people you
follow". Imported titles arrive in capitals (CARROT, SQUASH & PEAR SOUP).
"Cooks like you" suggests following ADMIN, who has shared nothing.

### 12. Tonight recommends cooking with cream five days past · decision

"Uses the extra thick double cream, already past" is the reason given for two
of the other ideas. The date is real (opened, keeps three days), not a guess.
Worth deciding how far past a thing can be before it stops being a reason to
cook it.

### 13. One place, several names · ~20 min

The shelf / Stock / Pantry; Recipes / Cookbook. The same destination is
called different things on different screens.

### 14. Finishing the shop waits on Nuremberg · ~30 min

"Got everything" and "Put these away" do nothing visible until the server
answers - no optimistic tick, just a faded button. Against the house rule
that anything which waits on a round trip should move first.

### 15. Small ones · ~20 min together

- The recipe page shows the whole source URL wrapped over three lines rather
  than "bbcgoodfood.com".
- "cooked 0×" on a recipe nobody has cooked.
- The header says "28 items" on /pantry while its own title says 31.
- "open" means two things on stock rows: "150ml open" (in the open pack) and
  "Unsalted butter open" (it has been opened).
- The paste page puts three paragraphs of explanation above the box.

## Checked and sound

- No page scrolls sideways at phone width; no page throws.
- Cook mode has a Back button; it was hidden behind Next's dev badge in the
  first screenshots, not missing.
- Desktop has its tab bar; an early screenshot caught the segmented control
  mid-fade.
- The FAB sheet, the plan-a-day sheet, the planner's slot sheet and the bulk
  bar all sit on top of everything as they should.
- Login, and a signed-out visit redirecting with `?next=`.
